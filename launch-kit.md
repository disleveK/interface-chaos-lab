## LinkedIn launch post

Your interface looks polished. Now let reality use it.

I just open-sourced **Interface Chaos Lab**, an AI skill that stress-tests web products against the failures demos rarely show: expired sessions, offline saves, duplicate submissions, tiny screens, keyboard-only navigation, stale data, localization, and more.

It generates a deterministic “Disaster Roulette” plan, captures reproducible evidence, assigns severity, calculates a survival score, and can replay the exact same storm after a fix.

I tested the first release on OncoSys AI. It caught conflicting Q2/Q3 pilot language and a duplicated skip-navigation link, while confirming that the readiness demo blocks duplicate submissions correctly. The honest result: 91/100 with 4 of 7 scenarios executed; unsupported production injections stayed BLOCKED, not magically marked as passes.
